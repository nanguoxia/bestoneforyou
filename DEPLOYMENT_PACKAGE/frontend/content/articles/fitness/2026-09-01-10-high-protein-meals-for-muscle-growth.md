# 10 High-Protein Meals to Support Muscle Growth

![Featured Image](https://trae-api-cn.mchost.guru/api/ide/v1/text_to_image?prompt=A%20healthy%20meal%20prep%20spread%20with%20grilled%20chicken%2C%20salmon%2C%20eggs%2C%20tofu%20and%20colorful%20sides%20in%20meal%20prep%20containers%2C%20bright%20kitchen%20counter%2C%20clean%20overhead%20food%20photography&image_size=landscape_16_9)

Muscle is built by training, but it is assembled from protein at meals. Hitting 1.6–2.2 grams per kilogram of body weight daily is far easier when you rotate a handful of go-to meals that each deliver 30–50 grams without culinary heroics. Here are ten.

## Breakfasts

**1. Greek Yogurt Power Bowl (≈40 g protein)**
200 g Greek yogurt, a scoop of whey or plant protein, mixed berries, 30 g granola, chia seeds. Zero cooking, five minutes, and the yogurt's casein keeps amino acids flowing for hours.

**2. Savory Egg Scramble (≈35 g)**
3 whole eggs + 100 g egg whites scrambled with spinach, peppers, and feta; whole-grain toast on the side. Egg whites stretch the protein without much added fat.

**3. Protein Oats (≈35 g)**
60 g oats cooked in milk with a scoop of protein powder stirred in off the heat, topped with banana and peanut butter. The classic breakfast of lifters everywhere — warm, cheap, endlessly variable.

## Lunches

**4. Chicken Burrito Bowl (≈45 g)**
150 g grilled chicken thigh over rice with black beans, corn, salsa, avocado, and a squeeze of lime. Beans and chicken stack two protein sources; one bowl covers half a training day's needs.

**5. Tuna & White Bean Salad (≈40 g)**
One can of tuna with cannellini beans, red onion, cherry tomatoes, olive oil, and lemon juice on greens. Shelf-stable ingredients, assembled in minutes — perfect for office lunches.

**6. Turkey Club Wrap (≈38 g)**
Whole-wheat tortilla, 120 g sliced turkey breast, light mayo, lettuce, tomato, and a slice of cheese. Toast the wrap for crunch; add an apple on the side.

## Dinners

**7. Salmon with Potatoes & Broccoli (≈42 g)**
180 g salmon fillet roasted at 200°C for 12–15 minutes alongside baby potatoes and broccoli. The fattiest protein here also brings omega-3s for recovery and heart health.

**8. Beef Stir-Fry with Rice (≈45 g)**
150 g lean beef strips flash-fried with peppers, snap peas, garlic, ginger, and soy sauce over jasmine rice. Cook the beef hot and fast to keep it tender.

**9. Tofu & Edamame Curry (≈35 g)**
300 g firm tofu (pressed, cubed, pan-crisped) simmered in curry sauce with edamame and vegetables over rice. Proof that plant-based dinners can hit serious protein numbers.

## Snack or Light Meal

**10. Cottage Cheese Bowl (≈30 g)**
250 g cottage cheese with pineapple or peach chunks and a sprinkle of nuts. Slow-digesting casein makes this the ideal pre-bed snack, feeding muscle repair overnight.

## Making It Work All Week

- **Cook in batches:** Grill 1 kg of chicken and a tray of salmon on Sunday; portion with rice and vegetables into containers
- **Anchor every meal with protein first**, then build the plate around it — never the reverse
- **Keep backups:** canned tuna, frozen shrimp, Greek yogurt, and protein powder rescue any short day in two minutes
- **Season boldly:** chili crisp, harissa, salsas, and spice blends keep ten meals from feeling like one

## The Bottom Line

You do not need exotic foods or precise recipes to build muscle — you need protein at every meal, repeated daily. Rotate these ten, batch-cook on Sundays, and let a well-fed kitchen do half the work of your training.
