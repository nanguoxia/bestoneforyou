## Building a Grooming Routine That Supports Your Training

### The 10-Minute Weekly System
1. **Pre-trim prep:** Clean and dry your beard; comb to align hairs.
2. **Trim:** Start with a longer guard and work down—you can always remove more, never less.
3. **Edge and detail:** Use a precision head for neckline and cheek lines.
4. **Clean tools:** Brush and oil blades to extend tool life.
5. **Post-care:** Apply a moisturizer or beard oil to reduce irritation.

**Nutrition tie-in:** Hydration and adequate protein intake support skin health. Aim for roughly 1.6–2.2 g of protein per kg of body weight daily if you train regularly, and drink water throughout the day—dehydrated skin is more prone to irritation.

## Realistic Timelines and Expectations

- **Better grooming habits:** Noticeable improvement in 1–2 weeks of consistency.
- **Skin adaptation:** 2–4 weeks for irritation-prone skin to adjust to regular trimming.
- **Tool lifespan:** Quality trimmers last 2–5 years with proper cleaning and oiling.

## Recovery and Injury Prevention for Grooming

- **Avoid trimming over irritated or sunburned skin.**
- **Disinfect blades** after any nick or cut.
- **Take a "skin rest day"** if you notice persistent redness.
- **Use proper lighting** to prevent uneven results and accidental nicks.
